Cross-Company Observations: Low-Cost Wins, High-Cost Disappointments
Looking across the year, the campaigns with acquisition costs under $10,000 tended to outperform those above $15,000, and the relationship was stronger than the team expected. Houston and Miami produced better ROI per dollar than Los Angeles, though the sample sizes were small enough that no one wanted to draw firm conclusions. Language turned out to matter more than anticipated: campaigns run in French and German outperformed English and Spanish on engagement, possibly because competition for attention was lower. Longer campaigns of 45-60 days generally beat shorter ones, with one exception: email, where a well-timed 15-day burst often matched the 60-day results at a fraction of the cost.
