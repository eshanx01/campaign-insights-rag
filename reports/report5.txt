Alpha Innovations: Social Media Overspend (Facebook, Chicago)
Alpha Innovations put $18,000 behind a 15-day Facebook campaign targeting women 35-44 with German-language ads. Impressions were low (under 1,800) but clicks were high at 861, meaning an unusually strong click-through rate. Conversion, however, stayed at 9%, and the acquisition cost was among the highest in the dataset. The campaign looks efficient on engagement and expensive on outcomes. One analyst commented that the audience was clearly interested but the checkout process lost them. ROI was 6.7x, so the campaign technically paid off. Still, several stakeholders questioned whether that was luck or repeatable performance.
