Innovate Industries: Influencer Misfire on Google Ads (Jan 2021)
This one did not go well. Innovate spent $17,500 over 60 days on an influencer campaign for the Foodies segment, then routed the traffic through Google Ads. Almost 9,000 impressions produced 659 clicks and only a 5% conversion rate. Engagement scored a dismal 1 out of 10, and post-campaign review found the influencer content and the landing experience had almost nothing in common. The Mandarin-language creative was reportedly a direct translation of the English script, with no localization, which likely hurt credibility. ROI came in at 3.6x. That is not a disaster on paper, but it fell well short of what the same budget earned on email.
