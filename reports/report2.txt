NexGen Systems: Women 35-44 Email Push, New York (Feb 2021)
NexGen's 60-day email campaign aimed at women aged 35-44 in New York was one of the strongest performers of the quarter. It converted at 12%, roughly double the email average, on an acquisition cost of about $11,500. ROI landed at 5.6x. Engagement scored 7 out of 10, and German-language variants outperformed the English control by a noticeable margin, which surprised the team given the small size of that audience. The Fashionistas segment responded best to subject lines built around limited-time seasonal offers. Leadership has asked that the German-language creative be reused in the Chicago rollout.
