DataTech Solutions: Display Ads Across Miami and New York
DataTech ran display campaigns targeting all age groups, and the results were inconsistent. In Miami, a 60-day YouTube placement reached the Health & Wellness segment at an 11% conversion rate and a 5.55 ROI, which the team considered solid. In New York, a 15-day Instagram display run aimed at Foodies collapsed to 100 clicks from 1,643 impressions, with an engagement score of 1. The likely cause was the short duration. Fifteen days was simply not enough for the algorithm to optimize. The lesson noted in the retro: display needs at least 45 days of runway, and anything shorter should be treated as a test budget, not a real campaign.
