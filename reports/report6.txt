TechCorp: Mixed Results Across Channels, Q1 2021
TechCorp's email campaign to men 25-34 in New York was the quiet star: 14% conversion, $9,975 in acquisition cost, and a 7.06 ROI, all on a 45-day run. Their Facebook social campaign for the same demographic, however, produced an engagement score of 10 but a conversion rate of only 9%, suggesting people loved the content but were not buying. And the Houston display campaign was cancelled after three weeks when click volume flatlined. Overall the company ended the quarter profitable, though the marketing lead admitted internally that email was doing most of the heavy lifting while other channels mostly generated noise.
