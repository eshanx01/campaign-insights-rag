Q3 Search Campaign — Southeast Asia (UA x JAL)
The Q3 paid search campaign targeting Southeast Asian markets underperformed
against target by 18%. Click-through rate was strong at 4.2%, but conversion
rate dropped to 1.1%, well below the 2.3% benchmark. Root cause analysis
pointed to landing page load time issues on mobile, which accounted for an
estimated 60% of the shortfall. A/B testing of a simplified mobile checkout
flow is recommended for Q4.
